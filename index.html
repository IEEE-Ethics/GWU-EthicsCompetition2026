About the competition

The Student Ethics Competition is organized by the GWU IEEE Student Branch with IEEE SSIT, the IEEE Society on Social Implications of Technology, and supported by the GWU Department of Mechanical and Aerospace Engineering. It encourages students to study professional ethics and to practice the kind of reasoning engineers need when technical, business and public interests pull in different directions.

Every team receives the same hypothetical case. Teams identify the ethical issues at stake, weigh the options open to the people involved, and recommend a course of action. All analysis and recommendations must be grounded in the IEEE Code of Ethics.

Format

  1. Receive the caseAll teams get the same case study and a copy of the IEEE Code of Ethics on October 04, 2026 at the 11:30 AM US EST.
  2. Prepare your analysisBuild a slide presentation that identifies the ethical issues, the stakeholders, the relevant clauses of the Code, and your recommendation.
  3. PresentDeliver your analysis in 8 to 10 minutes.
  4. DefendAnswer questions from the judges for 5 minutes. Presentations are open to attendees, but only judges may ask questions.

Judging

Judges evaluate how well each team identifies the ethical issues, applies the IEEE Code of Ethics, supports practical and well-reasoned recommendations, communicates clearly, and responds to questions. The decisions of the judges are final.

How to compete

Who can enter

  • Teams of up to three undergraduate students.
  • Open to students from GWU undergraduate students only..
  • IEEE Membership not required. Current IEEE student membership encouraged.

Sign up

If you are interested in competing as a Team (or as an individual Student), Submit the intent to compete form by October 02, 2026. Spots are limited to 8 teams and are filled in the order forms are received.

You must complete the Intent to Compete form to declare your intention to participate by the Intent to Compete Deadline.

If you are notified that your team has been accepted, your team members must Register for the conference by the Student Competition Registration Deadline or your team may be removed from the competition and any waitlisted teams will be given the opportunity to compete in your place.

There is no registration fees.
