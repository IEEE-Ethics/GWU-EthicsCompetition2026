    How it works
    
      
      
        
    • Every team gets the same engineering ethics case on the day.
      • 
        
    • About an hour to prepare slides, offline, in an assigned room.
      • 
        
    • 8–10 minute presentation, then 5 minutes of judges' questions.
      • 
        
    • Analysis must be grounded in the IEEE Code of Ethics.
      •
